What Gets Checked on Every Submission

The checklist is standardized so nothing important depends on who reviews your file.

CHK-01

Employee Information

Names, employee numbers, daily wage rates, and employment status match the company registry.

CHK-02

Attendance & Timekeeping

Days worked, unexcused absences, tardiness/undertime, and authorized leaves reconcile with logs.

CHK-03

Overtime & Premiums

Overtime hours match recorded excess hours, rest day work, and night shift differential rules (10%).

CHK-04

Mandatory Deductions

SSS (5% EE), PhilHealth (2.5% EE), and Pag-IBIG (₱200 EE) bracket calculations verified.

CHK-05

Holiday Pay Compliance

Regular holidays (200%) and special non-working holidays (130%) verified against DOLE calendar.

CHK-06

Payroll Reconciliation

Gross pay minus total deductions exactly equals Net Pay with itemized employee breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do monthly payments and approvals work?

Clients submit proof of subscription payment (via GCash or MariBank QR). VeriPay admin staff will inspect the reference number and mark the payment as VALID or INVALID. Once marked Valid, payroll file submission is instantly unlocked for your coffee shop.

What happens if I need to re-submit my payroll file after the admin sent the final report?

Once an admin operator sends the Final Payroll File, any re-submission of modified employee hours or corrections will incur an additional ₱350 recalculation fee per re-audit session. Please review and confirm your initial upload carefully!

Can I adjust my shop's payout schedule?

Yes. Every registered client can set and edit their payout frequency anytime in their dashboard: Monthly, Semi-Monthly (15th and 30th), or Weekly cycles.
